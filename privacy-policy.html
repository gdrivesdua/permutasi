Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Website: https://domainname.web.id/

1. Overview & Local Browser Processing

At Domain Name (domainname.web.id), protecting your privacy is fundamental to our utility design. All keyword inputs, pattern formulas, RFC 1035 validation checks, and domain permutation lists are computed directly inside your web browser.

We do not require user registration, accounts, or personally identifiable information to use the domain permutation generator. Your browser only stores minimal interface preferences (such as selected language preferredLanguage and light/dark theme preferredTheme) in localStorage.

2. Log Files & Edge Infrastructure

Like standard static web applications, our hosting and CDN infrastructure (such as Cloudflare) may process standard non-identifying HTTP request metadata—such as IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), timestamps, and referring URLs—strictly to ensure network security, DDoS mitigation, and reliable page delivery.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

To keep Domain Name free for everyone, we may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and Google-certified third-party ad networks.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as Google Advertising / DoubleClick cookies) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to users based on their visit to domainname.web.id and/or other sites across the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices.

4. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate traffic trends (such as page views, preferred language editions, and device types) so we can improve tool performance and usability. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage telemetry according to Google's Privacy Policy and does not capture private domain keyword brainstorming submitted offline or inside client state.

5. External Third-Party Links

Our domain inspection panel contains optional external research links to third-party platforms (including Ahrefs, Wayback Machine, Google Search, and DNSRobot). Once you click an external link and leave domainname.web.id, this Privacy Policy no longer governs your session. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those respective third-party websites. See also our Disclaimer.

6. GDPR (EEA/UK) & CCPA Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including residents of the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and California), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data collected by third-party advertising partners, as well as the right to object to or opt out of personalized cookie tracking through your browser settings or the opt-out links in Section 3.

7. Children's Information

Domain Name is intended for general developer, research, and business audiences and does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

8. Contact & Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes take effect immediately upon publication on this page. If you have questions about this policy, please visit our Contact Page, review our Terms of Service, or email admin@domainname.web.id.
